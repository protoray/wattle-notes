1. About Wattle Notes

Wattle Notes is an independent, free, open-source Markdown notebook maintained through the Wattle Notes GitHub project. References to the maintainer mean the maintainers of that project. It is mostly vibe coded with AI assistance, guided by the maintainer’s ideas and feedback.

The app works with files on your device. Local use needs no Wattle Notes account. The project does not provide a hosted note-storage service, and optional cloud connections are made from your device to the provider you choose.

Optional cloud encryption does not encrypt the local notebook. Keep your encryption passphrase and recovery key safe: the project has no account service that can reset them or recover a lost key.

4. Optional Google Drive connection

Google Drive is optional. In a configured build, you choose whether to sign in with Google and authorise access through Google’s consent screen. The app uses drive.file access for files it creates or that you make available to it, for notebook synchronisation and restoration. It does not require access to every file in your Drive.

Cloud note contents, tags, attachments and revision payloads are encrypted on your device before upload. Google can still see metadata such as filenames, folder structure, sizes and activity. The Privacy Policy explains storage, credentials and data handling in more detail.

You can disconnect in the app or revoke access in your Google Account connections. Disconnecting removes the saved Google refresh token; it does not delete your local notes or files already stored in Drive. Manage remote files through Google Drive. Deleting app-managed remote history can disrupt synchronisation, so keep a separate backup.

Google’s own terms, privacy rules, storage limits and availability also apply. Wattle Notes is independent of Google and is not endorsed by Google. The current desktop download includes the Wattle Notes Google client. No Google Cloud project or client import is required for users. Availability remains subject to Google’s account, consent and service policies.

5. Other providers and updates

Nextcloud / WebDAV is an optional, separate encrypted backup connection to a server you choose. Its operator’s terms and availability apply. Disconnecting leaves existing server backups in place; you manage their deletion with that provider.

Updater-enabled builds can check GitHub for new releases. You can disable startup checks and check manually instead. Downloading and installing an update requires your choice. GitHub, Google and other external services operate under their own terms; their availability and future compatibility are outside the project’s control.

6. Alpha status and support

Wattle Notes is an early alpha. Features may change, fail or become unavailable. The maintainer has tested Google sign-in, encrypted sync and restoration into a fresh folder. Conflict recovery, failure scenarios and optional Nextcloud backups still need live service validation. Current Mac downloads are unsigned by Apple and not notarized; other packaged platforms still need acceptance testing. The app has not had an independent security audit.

Start with a test notebook and keep independent copies of important writing. The project does not promise uninterrupted service, error-free operation, a particular release schedule or a support response time.
